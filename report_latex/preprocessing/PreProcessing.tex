

Preprocessing is deliberately kept lightweight, as most modeling decisions are driven by exploratory analysis. The textual input is obtained by concatenating the article title and body and converting the resulting text to lowercase, without aggressive cleaning, in order to preserve editorial and structural cues.

A limited set of numeric features is additionally introduced, including token counts, title and article lengths, and their ratio. These attributes capture stylistic properties of the articles and provide marginal but consistent improvements in linear models, while remaining complementary to the semantic information encoded in the text.
